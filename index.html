Orientation Group
A large-group information session delivered as the event unfolds or immediately after — sharing facts, normalizing reactions, and connecting people to next steps. Often delivered as a Town Hall, Staff Meeting, or Community Meeting.

Stabilization Group
A structured, 6-phase interactive group that helps participants name their experience, recognize their reactions, and build an immediate self-care plan. Offered once the incident itself has ended.

Coping Group
Our most comprehensive group intervention — a carefully structured 12-phase conversation that guides participants through their experience of the incident in a way that promotes genuine healing and recovery. Groups are intentionally composed in collaboration with your organization, accounting for each participant's level of exposure and impact. Participation is always completely voluntary, including once in the room.

Why organizations choose Riverside Trauma Center
Always expert-led
Every response is facilitated by a trained Riverside Trauma Center employee or representative. Trained and experienced in Psychological First Aid and Post-Traumatic Stress Management.
Evidence-based
Our interventions follow a specific, expertly curated and field-tested process — grounded in established frameworks adapted through decades of real-world practice.
Intentional by design
Participants in each group are intentionally hand-selected through a collaboration between your organization and ours — accounting for differing levels of exposure and impact to ensure the best outcomes.
Prioritizes autonomy
Group attendance is always completely optional and voluntary. So is participation. Once in the group, individuals are free to listen, reflect, and share only what feels right for them.
